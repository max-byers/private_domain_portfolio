Welcome to I Prescribe Exercise

I spent years training through pain instead of training around it, and it cost me more time on the sidelines than any single hard workout ever earned me. I'm not credentialed. I learned anatomy, rehab protocols, and program design the hard way, working through my own overuse injuries with physical therapists. Every week I send one honest lesson on avoiding the mistake I already made, so you don't have to.
